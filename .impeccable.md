## Design Context

### Users

AniDiagram serves developers and technical content creators who need to explain
systems quickly, evaluate a diagram at a glance, and reuse the result in docs,
presentations, or product surfaces.

### Brand Personality

Professional, clear, and lively. The diagrams should feel technically credible
without becoming sterile, and expressive without becoming noisy.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the `illustrated-character-v1` icon family as the recognizable visual
signature inside a disciplined technical-diagram hierarchy. Prefer a strong
focal node, meaningful spatial grouping, and restrained accent color. Avoid
generic neon-AI decoration, equal-weight card grids, and simultaneous motion
that competes for attention.

### Design Principles

- Establish one narrative focus before adding detail or motion.
- Keep node shells and layout stable; let character internals carry expression.
- Use scale, position, and grouping to make reading order obvious without prose.
- Coordinate motion as a sequence, with limited concurrent edge activity.
- Preserve `prefers-reduced-motion` behavior and readable static states.
- Keep README previews lightweight, smooth at 24 fps, and visually seamless when
  they loop.
